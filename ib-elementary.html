The Approach

What the IB PYP actually teaches.

Self-directed from the start.

Children drive their own projects with teachers as guides, not as answer-keys. They learn to take responsibility for their own thinking.

Subjects without walls.

Transdisciplinary learning dissolves the lines between math, language, science and the arts, the way real problems do.

The classroom leaves the building.

Field trips to quarries, museums, rivers and working professionals. Community is where the learning gets tested.

Two languages, one programme.

The full PYP delivered in English or French, with Arabic throughout.

The PYP Exhibition

Every year ends with proof.

Each student spends nine weeks on a real-world inquiry of their own choosing, then presents it to families and the whole school. The PYP Exhibition is where independent thinking becomes something you can watch.
