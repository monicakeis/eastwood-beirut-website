Photography Shot List

Every image slot on the site, keyed to the exact file it replaces.

Forty photography frames plus one motion clip. Shoot to the aspect ratio listed, name each delivery with the exact target filename in the File column, and it drops straight into place. Real-people frames (Head of School and staff portraits) are intentionally excluded — those stay as-is.

Global art direction — applies to every frame
  • Palette: warm natural light; let the brand deep-blue (#000077) and teal (#00B5AD) appear via uniforms, walls, props where it reads honestly. No heavy filters.
  • Feel: candid and in-the-moment, not posed line-ups. Eye contact optional; activity is the subject.
  • No readable text or third-party logos in frame (jerseys, brands). Keep clothing plain.
  • No identifiable staff/adults as the subject — children lead every frame. An adult may appear soft/out-of-focus in support only.
  • Heroes: leave generous negative space (upper-left / left third) for a headline overlay. Keep faces out of the lower-left text zone.
  • Resolution: deliver at 2× the minimums listed; sRGB, high quality JPG (heroes) or PNG. We compress on ingest.
  • Framing: shoot slightly wider than the crop so we can reposition; subjects placed a touch high (heroes crop to ~38–45% vertical).
  • Consistency: same lens character and grade across a page so a section feels like one shoot.
