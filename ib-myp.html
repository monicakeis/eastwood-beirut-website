The Approach

Four things the MYP does differently.

Concepts over memorization.

Learning is built around big ideas that transfer across subjects, not facts to be recalled.

Learning in context.

Six global contexts tie every subject to the real world, extending the transdisciplinary themes of the primary years.

Eight subjects, one framework.

From sciences and mathematics to design, the arts, and language acquisition in French or Arabic.

Learning how to learn.

Approaches to Learning run through every subject. The skills that make a lifelong learner.

The Personal Project

Every student ends with a project of their own.

In their final MYP year, each student designs and delivers a personal project: a self-chosen challenge, a finished product, and a written report, moderated by the IB itself. It is where independence becomes accomplishment, and the rehearsal for the Diploma's Extended Essay.
