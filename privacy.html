Privacy.

Questions?info@eastwoodschools.com

At Eastwood International School, the privacy of visitors to eastwoodis.com is a priority. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Consent

By using our website, you consent to this policy and agree to its terms.

Information we collect

When you contact us directly or submit a form, we may receive your name, email address, phone number and the contents of your message. The reason for asking for any personal information is made clear at the point we ask for it.

How we use it

To operate and maintain the website, to communicate with you, to respond to enquiries, to send relevant updates, and to improve how the website serves families.

Log files

Like most websites, we keep standard log files: IP address, browser type, internet service provider, date and time, and referring pages. This data is not linked to personally identifiable information and is used to analyse trends and run the site.

Cookies and storage

The website stores a few preferences on your device, such as the checklist ticks on the Admissions page and the language you chose on the Section Française page. They stay on your device and are not sent to us. You can clear them, or turn off cookies, in your browser settings.

Your rights

You can ask to access your personal data, to correct or erase it, to restrict or object to its processing, and to have it transferred. Contact us and we will respond within one month.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the website, contact us and we will remove it promptly.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect once posted on this page.
