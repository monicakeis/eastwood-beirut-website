Privacy Policy.

At Eastwood Beirut International School, accessible from eastwoodis.com, the privacy of our visitors is a priority. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, and how we use it. If you have questions, contact us at info@eastwoodschools.com.

Consent

By using our website, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

Information we collect

When you contact us directly or submit a form, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, and the contents of your message. The reasons for requesting any personal information are made clear at the point we ask for it.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to operate and maintain our website, to communicate with you, to respond to enquiries, to send relevant updates, and to improve how the website serves families.

Log files

Like most websites, we use log files. These record standard data such as IP address, browser type, internet service provider, date and time, and referring pages. This data is not linked to personally identifiable information and is used to analyze trends and administer the site.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to store visitor preferences and pages accessed, in order to improve the experience. You can disable cookies through your browser settings.

Your data protection rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, to request correction or erasure, to restrict or object to processing, and to request data portability. To exercise any of these rights, contact us and we will respond within one month.

Children's information

Protecting children online is a priority. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through our website, contact us and we will remove it promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes are effective once posted on this page.

Contact

For any questions about this policy, contact info@eastwoodschools.com.
