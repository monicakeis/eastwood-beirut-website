Ages 3–6
The Approach

How we teach the early years.

Play is the work.

In the early years, play is how a child thinks, tests, and understands the world. We treat it as seriously as they do.

We start with the child.

Every unit of inquiry begins with what a three-year-old actually wonders about. Curiosity first, curriculum around it.

Two languages from day one.

English and French sections, each adding Arabic and the second language early, through song, conversation, and movement, not drills.

Montessori-grounded, IB-aligned.

A prepared environment and self-directed discovery, inside the structure of the IB Primary Years Programme.

The Day

What fills a child's day here.

Languages

French and Arabic for the English section, English and Arabic for the French section, taught through song and play.

Performing Arts

Music, drama, and public speaking that build confidence and voice.

Physical Education

Core movement skills and safe, cooperative play.

Story Time and Circle

Daily reading and dialogue tied to the unit of inquiry.

Monthly Field Trips

Inquiry taken out into the real world.

Social-Emotional Learning

Self-awareness, emotional regulation, and empathy, developed through age-appropriate daily practice.

Music and Movement

A daily practice for spatial awareness and motor skills.

Learning, in the early years, should look a lot like joy.
