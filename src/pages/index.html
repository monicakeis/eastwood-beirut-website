      Safe. Seen.
      Held.

      Before curriculum, before outcomes, you want to know your child is safe and seen. We've built our campus around that answer.

      • Gated, guarded, always.

        A Child Protection Policy developed with Himaya, in line with UNICEF standards and Lebanese law. A licensed nurse and counsellor on campus. An evacuation plan with two drills a year. Protocols reviewed every year.

      • Mental health is built in.

        Social-emotional learning is part of the daily curriculum from age 3, with a full-time counselling team.

      • Protected by policy.

        Clear reporting channels through the Child Protection Team, and an Inclusion Policy that protects every learner. Staff trained at orientation and through the year.

      • A genuinely international community.

        Inclusion support in every classroom, a multi-faith calendar, and a welcome for Lebanese and expatriate families alike.
