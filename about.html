We treat education
as a science. And learning as a calling.

Eastwood International School is an IB World School in Mansourieh that bridges the gap between educational theory and its relevance to real life.

Our mission

To impact the world by teaching students to balance mind, body and soul, and to couple knowledge with integrity, ambition with compassion, and achievement with service.

Our vision. To be a leading educational community in the Middle East for students of every nationality and faith, committed to continuous improvement and the highest standards of international education.

The years
that made us.

  1. 1973

    Eastwood begins.

    Amine M. Khoury founds Eastwood in Kafarshima, the first school of what is now Eastwood Schools.

    Eastwood Schools
  2. 1983

    Mansourieh opens.

    The campus on the hill above Beirut, home to Eastwood International School ever since.

  3. 2011

    Learning goes online.

    Online learning with Google and Apple, years ahead of most schools. The same year, the school is first accredited through Cognia.

  4. 2016

    An IB World School.

    Authorised by the International Baccalaureate for the Primary Years Programme and the Diploma Programme, and accredited again through Cognia.

  5. 2026

    Best School to Work.

    Platinum, from T4 Education, for the place our teachers come to work every day.
