About Eastwood International School

We treat education as a science.

And learning as a calling.

A leading IB World School in Mansourieh, where research-backed teaching meets real life.

Mission

To impact the world by teaching students to balance mind, body, and soul, and to couple knowledge with integrity, ambition with compassion, and achievement with service.

Vision

To be a leading educational community in the Middle East for students of every nationality and faith, committed to continuous improvement and the highest standards of international education.

Our Approach

Bridging theory and real life.

Eastwood is built on holistic, internationally proven frameworks, from Montessori in the early years through the full IB Continuum. Every activity has one purpose: to close the gap between what is learned in a book and how it lives in the world.

Officially accredited & recognized by the bodies that certify educational legitimacy worldwide.
