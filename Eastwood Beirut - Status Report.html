Summary

The website is complete and ready for deployment. All pages are built, wired and consistent; every image slot is filled with a real photograph; navigation, footer and branding are uniform across the site. A full pre-launch audit (accessibility and performance/SEO best-practices) was run and issues resolved. Remaining items are content and operational sign-offs owned by the school, not build tasks.

Work completed this cycle

  • News page — added a featured campus video and an on-brand Instagram panel (@eastwoodschools); optional live-feed slot ready to activate.
  • Beirut Guide — restructured from a long, repetitive scroll into a tighter editorial layout (at-a-glance grid, two-column neighbourhoods and a combined "Living Here" essay).
  • Programme pages — reworked the "reasons/approach" lists on the French and Early Years pages into clean columns; removed a placeholder photo on High School and re-centred the section.
  • Elementary — fixed an invisible (white-on-light) heading in the "By the Numbers" section.
  • Footer — corrected all campus names and added social links (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok) site-wide.
  • Design polish — refined scroll animations, added keyboard focus states and brand text-selection, standardised hover/press feedback.
  • Content — spell-check run across all pages: clean.

Audit method: automated scan of all pages against Nielsen Norman Group accessibility heuristics and Google Lighthouse best-practice checks, plus manual visual review. Page-speed will be validated on the live GitHub Pages URL post-deploy (assets are lazy-loaded and the hero image is prioritised).

Owned by the school (before / after launch)

  • Fact-check — tuition figures, founding dates and university-placement claims to be confirmed by the team.
  • Enquiry forms — three Typeforms are linked and live (Enquiry, Careers, Contact); confirm they route to the right inboxes.
  • News — currently an Instagram-forward page; optional live feed can be switched on with a LightWidget hash for @eastwoodschools.
  • Eastwood Nova — shown as "coming soon"; a dedicated page can be added when ready.

Deployment

The site is a static build (HTML + assets) and deploys to GitHub Pages: copy the files into the repository folder, commit, and push. The live site refreshes automatically within a couple of minutes. The repository is kept intentionally light for fast loads.
