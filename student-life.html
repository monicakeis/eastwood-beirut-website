Student Life

School is more than class.

It is where character is built.

Wellbeing, sport, the arts, and a campus that runs on care. The hours around the lessons matter just as much.

Wellbeing

A school that looks after its own.

Weekly social-emotional learning and yoga are part of the rhythm, not an add-on. A full-time nurse is on campus every day, and the grounds are gated, secure, and monitored, so students are free to focus on growing up.

Activities & Athletics

Something for every kind of student.

Clubs in music, dance, and science change each term. Beyond the gates: the Duke of Edinburgh International Award, an after-school football team, quarterly hikes, Saturday bike trips, and the yearly snow camp.

Getting Here

A safe ride, tracked in real time.

A fleet of minibuses serves families within roughly 15 kilometres of campus, 10 to 12 seats each, with bus monitors aboard. Parents follow their child's bus live through the NavLeb app, with arrival and departure alerts.

The lessons end at the bell. The learning does not.
