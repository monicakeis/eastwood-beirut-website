School is more
than class.

Wellbeing, sport, the arts and a campus that runs on care. The hours around the lessons matter as much as the lessons.

Everyday.week.term.quarter.year.

0105

  1. Every day

    • A full-time nurse on campusEvery school day, all day.
    • Gated, secure, monitored groundsSo students are free to get on with growing up.

  2. Every week

    • Social and emotional learningPart of the rhythm of the week, not an add-on.
    • YogaWeekly, alongside the timetable.
    • An after-school football teamFor students who want more of the game.
    • Saturday bike tripsBeyond the gates, on the weekend.

  3. Every term

    • Clubs that change each termMusic, dance and science among them.

  4. Every quarter

    • HikesOut of the classroom and into the hills.

  5. Every year

    • Snow campOnce a year, in the mountains.
    • The Duke of Edinburgh's International AwardA recognised framework for service, skills, fitness and adventure.

A safe ride,
tracked live.

A fleet of minibuses serves families across the Metn, each with a monitor aboard. Parents follow the bus in real time in the NavLeb app, with an alert when it arrives and when it leaves.

  • 15kilometres, roughly, the area the buses cover around campus
  • 10to 12 seats on each minibus
  • 1monitor aboard every bus
